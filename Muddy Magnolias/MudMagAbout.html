About Muddy Magnolias

Muddy Magnolias Vendor Market is a premier upscale boutique and vendor market located in Vicksburg, MS, offering a vibrant and eclectic shopping experience. We feature a wide array of local merchants, artisans, and independent vendors.

Shoppers can explore numerous booths offering a wide variety of goods, including:

  • •Boutique Items: Boutique clothing, shoes, and jewelry.
  • •Handmade crafts: Unique, locally crafted items such as handsewn bags, table runners, coasters, and handmade soaps.
  • •Gifts & Home Goods: A variety of seasonal decor, dip mixes, and unique souvenir items.
  • •Vintage Finds: An eclectic variety of vintage finds.
