About Me

I am a web developer with a passion for making websites that are classic in style but contemporary in use.

I spent 16 years in secondary education, where I taught math and computer science. After listening to my own teaching I have decided front end web development is what I want to do for work.

I have a wonderful family. My wife Lauren and I married in 2012; we have three children Jewel, Noah, and Gemma. I am an elder at Covenant Presbyterian Church (Chattanooga, TN), where I spend as much time as I can serving the youth.

I also coach cross country and track & field, so I know a thing or two about motivating people to accomplish greatness, even if it's difficult.

Work Experience

Computer Science Teacher at Ridgeland High School (2016-2021): I taught intro to digital technology, computer science, web design, and web development to students who did not have regular access to such an education. I started the program there-enabling students to finish computer science pathways in high school. Some students were able to immediately generate income by making websites for local people. Others even went on to college to graduate with computer science degrees. Unfortunately, the program ended at Ridgeland when I moved to Ringgold, which tells how difficult it was to make this program happen.

eSports Coach at Ridgeland High School (2018-2021): I started an eSports team at Ridgeland High School. The students played League of Legends, Rocket League, and Super Smash Bros. I specialized in League of Legends, where we spent hours practicing mechanics, scrimmaging, and strategizing future matches. One student even went to college by an eSports scholarship.

Math Teacher at Ringgold High School (Ringgold, GA), Ridgeland High School (Rossville, GA), and Louisville High School (Louisville, MS) (2010-present): I taught Algebra I, Algebra II, geometry, financial math, and statistical reasoning.

Cross Country Coach at Ringgold High School (2017-present): At Ringgold I coached the girls to a region championship and the boys to their highest team finish at state since at least the 90s. During this time I also saw Ridgeland go to state as a team for the first time in a very long time.

Track & Field Coach at Ringgold High School (2024-present): I coached a junior to a state runner-up finish this past year. I have also seen 10 school records fall as one of two head coaches. One student also won a state championship in high jump and was offered a scholarship to the University of Georgia!

Assistant Band Director at Ridgeland High School and Louisville High School (2010-2019): I taught students how to march and how to play brass instruments.

Engineering Aide at Applied Research Associates (2006-2008): Most of our work was contracted by the Air Force, where we visited bases and wrote massive reports for the Air Force to understand their concrete situations. This included what was working, what was not working, why it was not working, how to fix it, and a plan for the next ten years to keep it working properly. These reports were also sent to Congress for funding requests.
